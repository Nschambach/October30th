Design Choices

I chose a white background with the Chicago Cubs blue and red as my main colors for the text and header. I chose to make a chart about Dogs vs. Cats because I have a dog and a cat and it would be an easy way for my to have two pictures included. The charts are separated into individual sections to make the information easier to understand. I used contrasting colors to distinguish between data categories and organized the content to make the website easy to navigate.
